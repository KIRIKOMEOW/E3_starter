# Portrait Plans

## A - Geometry

I will make a funny alien face using over 100 small circles. The circles will form freckles on the cheeks and forehead. The face will look colorful and playful.

## B - Assets

I will make a cat explorer portrait using two images. One image will show cat ears above the head, and the other will show a fish-shaped badge on the cheek. I will draw both images myself as SVG files.

## C - Color and Curves

I will make a sleepy moon face. The head will have a gradient from purple to light blue. I will use Bezier curves for the eyes and mouth to create a calm expression.

## Selection Criterion

I will choose the portrait that looks the clearest and most interesting at a small size.
